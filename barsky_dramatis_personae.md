# Dramatis Personae: The Barsky Family & Associated Entities

*Prepared in connection with the analysis of Barsky family property transactions in Princeton, NJ, and their relationship to the residential tear-down/replacement trend documented in the 2023 Princeton Reexamination Report.*

---

## I. Roman Barsky's Branch — R.B. Homes / Barsky Enterprises

### Key Individuals

**Roman Barsky** *(aka Roman D. Barsky, Roman B. Barsky)*
Principal and founder. Emigrated from Odessa with his family after high school. Founded [R.B. Homes, Inc.](https://www.rbhomesonline.com/about-us) in 1987/1995 (sources differ). Has built over 200 custom homes in the Princeton area over more than three decades. Operates from offices at 152 Witherspoon Street, Princeton. Personally oversees design and construction on all projects.

**Emma Barsky** *(aka Emma A. Barsky)*
Wife of Roman. Co-signatory on numerous deeds and mortgages alongside Roman. Former personal residence at 18 Katies Pond Road; co-owner of 15 Katies Pond Road (held as investment/development land).

**Daniel Barsky** *(aka Daniel L. Barsky, Daniel Lawrence Barsky)*
Son of Roman and Emma. *Lawrence is Daniel's middle name — "Lawrence Barsky" and "Daniel L. Barsky" in county records refer to the same person.* [Graduated from Bryant University](https://www.rbhomesonline.com/about-us) with a degree in Business and Finance; joined the family business as a laborer and worked up to full partner. Now the primary client-facing partner and responsible for all aspects of R.B. Homes from land acquisition through sales. Also president of [Barsky Property Management LLC](https://www.barskymanagement.com/about). Personal residence during the CSV period was 220 Valley Road, Princeton (sold 2023); prior residence at 18 Katies Pond Road.

**Olga Barsky**
Wife of Daniel. Co-owner on all Daniel's property deeds (7 Hamilton Ave, 220 Valley Rd, 259 Edgerstoune Rd). Licensed real estate agent at [Keller Williams Real Estate — Princeton](https://kwprinceton.yourkwoffice.com/our-agents); serves as listing agent for R.B. Homes–built properties, functioning as the family's in-house sales channel.

**Simone Barsky** *(aka Simone Victoria Barsky)*
Daughter of Roman and Emma (likely). Appears only in two PPS (Pending Property Sale) affidavit records from 2003 and 2008 with no associated block, lot, or address. No active role in development operations confirmed.

## II. Igor Barsky's Branch — Heritage Homes Builders

Roman's younger brother Igor runs a **parallel but distinct** development operation under the Heritage Homes Builders brand. The two branches share the **Barsky Brothers Holdings LLC** entity for joint projects (most notably Barsky Court at 302 Nassau Street) but otherwise operate separately.

### Key Individuals

**Igor Barsky** *(aka Igor L. Barsky, Egor Barsky)*
Younger brother of Roman. [Came to America in his teens from the former Soviet Union](http://heritagehomesprinceton.com/about-us/), married his high school sweetheart, and established Heritage Homes Builders alongside his two sons Lawrence and Robert. Former Princeton addresses include 297 Moore Street and 306 Alexander Street. Has since relocated to Florida (Coral Gables / Pinecrest area). Also listed as CEO of Allgenic Health and Wellness.

**Alla Barsky** *(aka Alla L. Barsky, Alla B. Barsky)*
Wife of Igor. Co-owner on Princeton properties including 297 Moore Street and 10 Windermere Way. Phone records and public records confirm Alla's contact information is shared with Igor and their son Lawrence, and her Princeton address was 10 Windermere Way. Has relocated to Florida with Igor.

**Lawrence Barsky** *(aka Lawrence Igor Barsky)*
Son of Igor and Alla — **not** a child of Roman. [Heritage Homes Builders was established by Igor "along with his two sons: Lawrence and Robert."](http://heritagehomesprinceton.com/about-us/) Lawrence's middle name Igor appears in records (Lawrence Igor Barsky). In the county deed CSV, "BARSKY LAWRENCE" refers to this individual. He purchased 616 Ewing Street, Princeton (Block 5408, Lot 20) in 2014 and sold it in 2019. The email address `lawrencebarsky@gmail.com` appears in Igor's own public records, consistent with father–son shared business use.

**Robert Barsky**
Son of Igor and Alla; second principal of [Heritage Homes Builders](http://heritagehomesprinceton.com/about-us/). Co-owner with Alexandra Dahlin Barsky on 251 Colt Street, Hopewell Township (Block 78.37 Lot 1 C251), purchased October 2020 and sold April 2022.

**Alexandra Dahlin Barsky**
Wife of Robert Barsky (likely). Appears on all deeds with Robert, including the Hopewell Township property. The retained "Dahlin" surname suggests a hyphenated or partially retained maiden name.

## VI. A Note on 92–94 Spruce Street

The property at 92–94 Spruce Street (**Block 30.03, Lot 64**, per Daniel Barsky's own May 2025 correspondence) does **not appear in the CSV dataset**, which only captures 86 Spruce Street (Block 30.03 Lot 100, per the CSV's Block field — note the Block numbering discrepancy with the OPRA correspondence, which states Lots 62+63 for 86 Spruce and Lot 64 for 92–94 Spruce).

92–94 Spruce is currently managed by [Barsky Property Management](https://www.barskymanagement.com/property/92-94-spruce-st) as a duplex. Together with 86–88 Spruce, [the two parcels were rezoned by Princeton Council in February 2026 to the new "AH-12" zone](https://wolfhelius.github.io/monster-on-spruce-street/), created exclusively for these Barsky Enterprises–owned lots, permitting 30 new residential units (8 affordable) for a total of 42 units across 0.66 acres.

**Action required:** Search Mercer County deed records specifically for **Block 30.03 Lot 64** to find the acquisition instrument for 92–94 Spruce Street.
